# Hoizr Public Site Product and Content Refresh

**Date:** 23 June 2026  
**Package:** `business-client`  
**Status:** Approved design  
**Availability source:** `../docs/HOIZR_FEATURE_INVENTORY.md`, audited 21 June 2026

## Objective

Make the Hoizr organiser website production-ready by aligning every public claim
with the actual product, retaining the existing visual identity, and rebuilding
the content around a clear sales journey:

1. identify the organiser's problem;
2. show the commercial or operational consequence;
3. explain the Hoizr outcome;
4. demonstrate how the current product delivers it;
5. give the visitor a direct action.

The main promise is to help Indian event organisers sell more tickets and build
an audience that returns for future events.

## Confirmed Decisions

- Hoizr is an India-only product for this positioning pass.
- The audience includes organisers of parties, clubs, concerts, festivals,
  comedy, free and RSVP events, exclusive events, non-exclusive events and
  similar live experiences.
- The existing visual identity remains: typography, colour, motion, metallic
  imagery, laptop-frame hero and selected nightlife photography.
- The existing hero headline remains:

  > Fill the room.  
  > Keep the room.

- The logo remains standalone. No permanent brand tagline will be added.
- The primary CTA is `Get Started`.
- The secondary CTA is `Book a Demo`.
- Public content uses Indian/British English, including `organisation`.
- Public copy uses `event organisers`, `your audience` and `customer
  relationships`. `Fan CRM` may be explained deeper in product copy but is not
  a primary headline term.
- AI is a supporting capability, not the main product identity.
- The site may use confident category language, but comparative leadership
  claims must be objectively defensible.
- No testimonials, customer logos, invented public performance figures or
  unsupported social proof will be added.
- Do not discuss commissions or marketing-credit costs.
- The approved free-access claim is:

  > Free signup. No setup fee. No subscription. No card details required.

- Customer-facing channels may be named: WhatsApp, email, SMS, push and
  Instagram. Payment and infrastructure providers stay unnamed.
- Current and Coming Soon capabilities must always be visibly distinguished.
- The existing Beliefs ideas and conviction remain, but founder-specific
  sections are removed.

## Benchmark Findings

The benchmark set is SortMyScene, District, SkillBox, BookMyShow, Townscript,
Insider-style platforms and Swiggy Scenes, with Owner.com used as the content
quality benchmark.

### Indian event platforms

The reviewed Indian platforms mainly lead with consumer discovery, event
inventory, category browsing or generic calls to create/list an event.
BookMyShow's organiser page lists broad services, but does not build a tight
problem-to-outcome sales narrative. This leaves space for Hoizr to position
itself as the organiser's operating and growth platform rather than another
event directory.

### Owner.com

Owner.com provides the relevant structural benchmark:

- lead with the customer's business outcome;
- organise products by growth outcomes;
- describe the problem before the feature;
- use direct, short headlines;
- connect every capability to a commercial result;
- repeat a clear next action at high-intent points.

Hoizr will adopt this content discipline without copying Owner.com's claims,
proof model, visual system or restaurant terminology.
